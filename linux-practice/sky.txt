 good day
 A good day starts with a smile.
 I wake up early in the morning.
 I brush my teeth and take a bath.
 Then I eat a healthy breakfast.
 I get ready for school or work.
 I try to be kind to everyone.
 I listen carefully to others.
 I finish my work on time.
 I take small breaks when needed.
 I drink plenty of water.
 I eat healthy food during the day.
 In the evening, I spend time with my family.
 Sometimes, I play games with my friends.
 I also enjoy listening to music.
 At night, I finish my important work.
 I think about the good things in my day.
 Then I prepare for the next day.
 Finally, I go to bed with a happy mind.
